LLM post-training.
Code agents. RL.

I am Zichen Tian, a master's student in Artificial Intelligence at The Chinese University of Hong Kong and a research intern at Baidu, working on LLM post-training, code agents and reinforcement learning.

My research focuses on improving language models through supervised fine-tuning, reinforcement learning and distillation, with a particular interest in code agents. I work on verifiable training data, reward design and evaluation for repository-level software engineering and multi-turn tool use.

Previously, I collaborated with Huawei Hong Kong Research Center on tool-use agents and worked on LLM training and applications at Tencent and Zuoyebang.

Zichen Tian, Jinpeng Chen, Cheng Gong, Suiyun Zhang, Rui Liu
